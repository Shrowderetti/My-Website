About Me

Throughout my education, I have explored various ideas for my career path. I've always had an artistic side, which initially drove my interest in drawing, photography, and capturing the architecture around me.

With these passions in mind, I began studying Architecture at the Warsaw University of Technology. However, I soon realized that my true drive lay in spectrum of digital creation. This shift in perspective led me to getting a degree in Electronic Information Processing at the Jagiellonian University, allowing me to dive deep into computer graphics, programming, and data processing.

Currently, my main focus and absolute passion is 3D art. My goal is to work in Game Development preferably as a 3D environmental artist, though I am eager to embrace any experience in a such creative, technical environment. Here you will find a collection of my drawings, photos, 3D renders, and programming projects.
